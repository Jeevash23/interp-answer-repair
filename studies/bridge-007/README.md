## Design and interpretation

Four fixed candidate families, F10–F13, defined four permitted comparisons:
F10/F11, F10/F13, F11/F12 and F12/F13. All four families passed behavioral
screening; three pairs passed clean selection. The frozen ranking selected
F10/F11, whose weakest selection classification was 96.875%. It then passed
independent qualification on 1,152 configurations from 12 context blocks and
12 disjoint name triples. No alternative pair was substituted.

The six banks use 162 names absent from earlier project inputs and 53 unused
location/object combinations. F10/F11 supplies fresh confirmation within
related template structures, not broad syntactic generalization. Roles,
orders and wordings are paired observations within the crossed block/triple
sampling design, not additional independent sampling units.

The twelve historical S2 source heads, 176 later END measurement heads,
checkpoint, coordinate floor and zero/fit-mean replacement references were
fixed. All four recovery intervals were positive and all eight distance
margins favored the recipient. Wording-transfer top-1 accuracy rose from
33.9% corrupted to 90.7%, compared with 96.8% clean and 90.4% self transfer.

The four new prospective bounds were also supported: mean absolute donor-axis
discrepancy was 0.027/0.034 and full normalized signature discrepancy was
0.159/0.174 under zero/fit-mean references. Their adjusted upper bounds were
below the specified 0.10/0.50 resolutions. The sixteen primary quantities use
one 50,000-draw crossed-bootstrap interval family. These pooled average bounds
do not establish identical computations or absence of donor influence;
cellwise signed differences remain in the report. The original F6/F7
self-versus-wording analysis remains post hoc.
